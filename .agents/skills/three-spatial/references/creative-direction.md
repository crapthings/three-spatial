# Creative direction

Use for open-ended creation, sparse prompts, or a requested redesign. Develop the user's fragments into a coherent direction and actionable details. For a narrow repair, preserve the existing concept instead of starting a new one.

## Grow a concept from the brief

Separate explicit requirements from creative room. Extract useful cues from purpose, mood, setting, materials, forms, or interactions. Distinguish supplied facts from invented design choices; incomplete input is room to design, not a reason to ask the user to specify every detail.

Explore plausible interpretations when that helps, then choose a direction for relevance, distinctiveness, internal coherence, and feasibility. Do not require a fixed number of ideas or present brainstorming unless useful to the user. Combine cues through a meaningful relationship rather than accumulating unrelated motifs. Novelty should contribute to the requested experience.

Keep a concise working direction: what the scene expresses, its defining idea, and the choices that make that idea tangible. Use project configuration or a short task note when persistence helps; no new index fields or separate design document are required.

## Derive details through relationships

Develop primary forms and functional assemblies before decorative fragments. Ask what the chosen setting implies about use, construction, environment, history, or activity. Let those implications produce connected details: a use suggests access, access shapes circulation, and repeated activity leaves localized traces. Environmental storytelling can remain subtle or abstract; do not impose realism or a backstory on every brief.

Choose a few recognizable motifs and vary their expression across scales. Include contrast or surprise where it strengthens the concept. Preserve quieter areas and omit details with no useful role. Distinctiveness can come from silhouette, spatial relationships, or interaction as well as ornament.

For PCG, encode the chosen direction as related distributions and assembly rules. Randomness supplies variation within the concept; it does not select a new concept for every generated object.

## Move between invention and revision

Use [aesthetics.md](aesthetics.md) to assess how the concept is expressed, then refine or replace choices that weaken it. Keep decisions revisable as geometry and constraints become concrete. Stop expanding when the requested scope is fulfilled and additional ideas no longer strengthen it. Preserve user constraints and mark creative assumptions as choices rather than facts about a reference.
